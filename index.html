What it watches

Three things worth knowing about a wall.

A wall fails slowly, and it signals long before anyone sees a stain. I-Brick watches the three families of condition that matter, at the wall surface rather than in the middle of the room, and reports what they mean rather than what they read.

Environmental

Whether the wall is wetting, drying, or holding water it should have shed by now, and how it responds to the weather working against it. This is the signal that separates a wall recovering from one November storm from a wall that never recovers at all.

Thermal

How the wall performs against its own record, week on week. A section that starts losing more heat than it used to is usually a section that has taken on water, so the thermal signal corroborates the moisture one. It needs a real temperature difference across the wall to mean anything, and in mild weather we say so rather than guess.

Indoor

The conditions occupants are actually living in, and whether they track with what the wall is doing or diverge from it. A divergence tells you the problem is the building, not the household, which is usually the question in dispute.

The rule the product enforces: when we have not measured something, we say so. The node reads the air against the wall; the condition of the surface itself is modelled from that reading and labelled as modelled, never dressed up as a probe inside the masonry. A risk score that quietly treats “not measured” as “measured and fine” is worse than no score at all, so ours reports it as unassessed and moves on.

What it is worth

Cheap while it is still condensation. Expensive once it is a repair.

Envelope moisture is not a sudden event, it is a slow one that gets priced at the end. Every month it runs undetected moves it further up a cost curve that starts at ventilation and ends at replacing fabric, relocating an occupant, or arguing liability with a lawyer.

Catch it while it is still cheap

An alert while the wall is merely damp is a fan, a vent, or a drying schedule. The same wall eighteen months later is plaster, insulation, decoration and a displaced tenant. The average water damage claim runs to $15,400 before anyone has argued about cause.

Have evidence when the claim lands

Damp disputes turn on what the wall was doing and when. A continuous record answers that in an afternoon rather than an expert survey, whether you are defending a handover, a repairing obligation, or an insurance position.

Send people where the problem is

Blanket surveys cost the same whether or not they find anything. Ranked risk across a portfolio means the visit goes to the wall that needs it, and the other eleven wait.
